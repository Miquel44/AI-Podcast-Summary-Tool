# Solution Overview

After some tinkering and searching the web, this is the solution I landed on for the podcast submission. The podcast submission has a "Netflix-adjacent" style (I liked this more than the spotify list style, the images in the squares add a really nice touch for me).

## How it works (Architecture & Flow)
The flow is pretty simple to be honest. When you open the app, the first thing it does is load your interest profile( in this case the base format is my interest profile with some themes i chose that are of my liking like colombia today or tech) and use it to fetch  news, taking into account the topics you have in your profile and the language you have selected initially.

Then, it fetches news in a parallel way for speed from different sources (Google News rss, TechCrunch , The Verge, Hacker News, NPR, NYT, Financial Times, and local outlets like El País or El Tiempo). its a bit differet for the history section as Its not really a daily driver lets say, it generates the stories with prompts made by the ai mostly.

### Key architectural decisions
- **FastAPI + React** Fastapi for the backend and react for the frontend. I was more familiar with this stack as i had used it before so i just went with it.
- **SQLite -> SQLAlchemy:** zero setup for the demo.
- **APScheduler instead of Celery/queues:** one process is enough for a single-user demo, and the migration path to a real queue is straightforward.
- **Model choice:** GPT-5.6 Sol writes the scripts. I wanted to choose gemini 3.1 pro as i consider its the one that writes scripts and human like texts better but i dont have api access to pro, just for gemini flash, so it made sense to just use terra as its the better ranked in EQ creative writing. After that, the much cheaper GPT-5.6 Luna does the news curation, which is structured work and doesn't need a bigger model. In production id use either glm 5.3 flash as of today or deepsek v4 flash probably. Id have to test which is cheaper for this use case and if the quality difference is worth it.
- **RSS from different apis over scraping:** fully legal and free. The app extracts the whole text when possible to send to the scriptwriter, if not, it uses the summary to get better info and then produces the script from the summary and the knowledge the model has of the situation
- **Editions are cached per language and reused day to day while still relevant:**  The cost would scale by tags for topics and not as much with users, except for those with premium tiers that I explain in the other sections
- **Cost ledger:** every LLM/TTS call is logged in the real cost panel, and then theres a mock dashboard.

## The "Schedule" and Cloud Pre-generation Trade-off
Currently, the news get reset every 24 hours or when the app gets opened for the first time. In a real iteration of the  app id make it so it runs every hour or so, saving the relevant news generated and showing them to me and keeping a historic for all the podcasts which would eventually be sent to a s3 instace or similar for cheap storage.

For this demo, you  generate each episode manually by clicking on the news you are interested in. In a real production environment, I'd prefer that the episodes were pregenerated by topic so that the user could just click and listen to them. This saves a lot of money. the custom podcast would be a tab for the premium users.


## Customization & Features
- **Language Localization:** You can change the language whenever you want, and the app will generate news in that language. The results will be slightly different as the Google API is localized by language. The podcasts will then be generated and narrated in the chosen language.
- **Interests Tab:** You can select the topics you like, or even upload a voice note and the AI will extract the topics of interest from your audio.
- **Search:** You can also search for specific podcasts directly from the nav bar.

## Dashboard & Metrics
I built an internal dashboard where you can check mock statistics by episode or for the general usage of the app. This allows you to analyze different KPIs. I consider the most relevant metric for this product to be the **conversion rate**.

## Business Model & Monetization
The way I'd monetize an app like this is very specific through a tiered model:
- **Free Tier:** Listen to 3 short-form podcasts a day.
- **Mid Tier (Small fee):** Listen to 10 short podcasts and 3 long-form podcasts a day.
- **Premium Tier (Larger fee):** Unlimited listens. You can also interact by speaking or texting the podcasts to ask questions, which they would answer in voice. I'd also add a function to generate your own custom news (limited depending on the plan).

The core idea is that the more you pay, the more you can interact with the podcasts and the more you can generate your own custom content.

## Cost & Viability
The operation cost of the app is pretty low. The only real costs are the voice generation (ElevenLabs) and the LLM tokens for curation and scripts (OpenAI).
- The voice generation is a one-time cost per episode (so if you generate 10 episodes a day, it would be 10 times the cost of generating one episode) (~$0.90 per episode in voice plus ~$0.05 in LLM tokens, as measured by the app's own cost ledger in the dashboard).
- The news fetching  is free: everything comes from RSS feeds.

Overall, I think this is a very viable product that could be monetized in a way that makes sense for both the user and the business.
